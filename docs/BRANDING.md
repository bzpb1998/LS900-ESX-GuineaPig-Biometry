# OcularSpan identity

OcularSpan combines "ocular" (relating to the eye) and "span" (distance or extent). The short subtitle is **Biometry Research**. Neither is a claim of manufacturer affiliation or broader model validation.

The eye emblem was developed through AI-assisted concept editing, then redrawn as the small SVG used by the application. It uses a circular contour, flowing eyelid negative space and a separate central pupil. The app icon is rendered deterministically from src/ls900_biometry/assets/logo.svg; assets/brand-lockup.png is the selected wordmark presentation.

The brand is not presented as a registered trademark or as having received legal clearance. Device names belong in factual input-compatibility and methods descriptions, not the project wordmark. See PROJECT_NOTICE.md for the independent-project statement.

## Wordmark presentation prompt

Built-in image_gen.imagegen was used with the previously approved eye-logo presentation as the edit target, not a manufacturer's mark. The selected image is included in assets/brand-lockup.png.

Preserve the existing eye emblem exactly: circular black outer contour, natural slightly asymmetric white eyelid opening and separate central round pupil. Preserve its size and centered position.
Preserve the main wordmark "OcularSpan", but reduce its width slightly to be close to the width of the emblem, retaining refined regular-weight sans-serif lettering.
Replace the entire long subtitle "Independent Ocular Biometry Research Tool" with ONLY the exact two words "Biometry Research". Set them modestly sized and centered beneath OcularSpan with generous, precise spacing. No other text.
The whole identity should now feel more compact, restrained and elegant. Pure white background, flat black and white, same clean presentation. Do not add any badge, line, dots, colors, version number, manufacturer name, animals, shadow, texture or new graphic details. No change to the approved eye shape. One single logo.
